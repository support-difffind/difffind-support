Account & settings

Signing in, managing your AI provider connections, and what's saved where.

Do I need an account?

No. You can compare documents, including with the Heuristic AI provider, without signing in. Signing in mainly buys you one thing: your AI provider settings (which provider/model you use, and any API keys you've added) are saved to your account instead of just your current browser, so they follow you across devices and browser sessions.

Signing in with Google

Click Log in in the top right of the app. DiffFind uses Google sign-in only — there's no separate DiffFind password to create or remember. After you approve access, you're signed in and your name/avatar appear where the login button was.

Managing provider API keys

Open the gear icon to open Settings. Each real AI provider (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini) has its own section:

  • Add or replace a key — paste it into that provider's API key field. It saves automatically; there's no separate Save button.
  • Check what's connected — a configured provider shows a short hint instead of the full key, since the full key is never sent back to your browser once saved.
  • Remove a key — clear that provider's connection from the same panel to stop using it.

See where your key is stored and how it's protected for the details behind this.

Your default analysis settings

Settings also remembers which provider/model you last picked and whether semantic analysis is on by default, so you don't have to reconfigure it on every visit. Comparison options that aren't provider-related (Case sensitive, Normalize whitespace, Detect moves, Format, and the Show filters) are saved per-browser rather than per-account.

Signing out

Click Log out where your name/avatar appear. This ends your signed-in session on that browser; your saved provider connections and settings are still there the next time you sign back in.

Deleting your account or data

There's no self-service "delete account" button yet. To have your account, saved provider keys, and associated data removed, contact us from the email address associated with your account and we'll take care of it.
