## What's included

- DiffFind Free: deterministic lexical and structural comparison of pasted text, permanently free, no account.
- DiffFind Pro Trial: 90 days of every Pro feature starting at first launch — semantic and AI comparison (bring your own
  provider key), Semantic Find, loading from a URL or curl command, and PDF/Word/spreadsheet files.
- DiffFind Pro: unlock with a signed license key; verified offline.

Everything runs on your computer. See `docs/licensing.md` and `docs/installation.md`.

## Known limitations

- macOS Intel (x64): the local embedding engine ships no Intel-Mac binary, so Semantic Find is unavailable there until that is resolved.
- Auto-update is not enabled yet.
