Everything runs locally and offline. No account is needed, and no DiffFind server is contacted to check your license.

## The trial

The trial starts the first time you launch the app and lasts 90 days. The header shows the days remaining, and **Settings → License** shows the expiry date. When it ends, DiffFind switches to the Free tier. **Your documents, history, settings and API keys are never deleted** — Pro features are simply unavailable until you activate a license.

The trial record is stored on your computer, protected against accidental or casual edits, and guarded against the clock being set back. It is not tamper-proof, and DiffFind does not collect device fingerprints.

## Activating a license

1. Open **Settings → License** (or click the badge in the header).
2. Paste your license key and choose **Activate License**.

Keys look like `DFPRO1.…` and are verified offline against a public key built into the app. A key that is malformed, edited, signed by someone else or expired is rejected and changes nothing. Keys may be perpetual or time-limited; a time-limited key returns you to Free when it lapses.

## Moving to a new computer

Install DiffFind there and paste the same license key.
