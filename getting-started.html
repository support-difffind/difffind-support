Getting started with DiffFind

What it does, and how to run your first comparison in under a minute.

What is DiffFind?

DiffFind compares two pieces of content — text, JSON, YAML, XML, CSV, or PDF — and shows you exactly what changed. It's built in two layers:

  • Deterministic comparison runs first, always, and is never overwritten by AI output. It's the same character/word/line diff for plain text, and a structural, path-based diff for JSON/YAML/XML/CSV — so reordering keys or reformatting whitespace never shows up as a false change.
  • AI semantic analysis is optional and runs on top of the deterministic result. It explains what a change means in plain language (a deadline changed, a requirement was removed, a value increased) — and every factual value it cites is checked against your source documents before it's shown as verified. See AI Semantic Analysis.

Your first comparison

  1. Open app.diffind.com.
  2. Paste your original content into the Before panel, and the updated content into After.
  3. Click Compare.

That's it — no account or API key is required. DiffFind auto-detects the format (JSON is checked first, then XML, then YAML, then CSV, falling back to plain text), so you don't need to tell it what you pasted.

Want an AI explanation of the changes, not just the raw diff? Turn on semantic analysis in the Analysis dropdown before comparing — see AI Semantic Analysis for how to pick a provider.

Loading content without copy-pasting

Each Before/After panel has a loader dropdown (default Plain) so you don't have to hand-copy content:

  • URL — fetches a GET request and loads the response body.
  • curl — paste a full curl command; it's parsed and executed for you, and the response body is loaded.
  • File — reads a local file directly in your browser, including .pdf files.

Full details, including how PDF comparison works, are in Comparing Documents.

Reading the results

After you compare, you'll see two things:

  • An inline diff view — your two documents side by side, line-numbered, with changed lines tinted and the exact changed characters darkened within each line.
  • A list of change cards below it, one per change, each showing its type (addition, deletion, modification, or move) and — if semantic analysis is on — an AI interpretation, severity, and confidence. Click a card to jump straight to that change in the panes above.

Use the Show filter bar to hide/show change types, and the find bar above the panels to search the raw text you're about to compare.
