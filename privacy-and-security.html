Privacy & security

What DiffFind does and doesn't store, and how your API keys are protected.

This page describes how the product is built, not a legal privacy policy. For the full policy and terms, or for anything not covered here, contact us.

Your compared documents

The Before/After content you compare is sent to DiffFind's server to run the comparison, and — if you've turned on semantic analysis with a real AI provider — passed to that provider to generate an interpretation. It is not stored server-side beyond what's needed to serve that response.

History and your comparison options are kept in your browser's local storage only, not on DiffFind's servers — clearing your browser data clears them too.

Files and PDFs

Files you load with the File loader, including PDFs, are read directly in your browser — the raw file is not uploaded anywhere to be loaded into the comparison. A PDF's pages are rendered and its text extracted locally in your browser via pdf.js; that extracted text is what gets sent for comparison, the same as if you'd pasted it. A History entry sourced from a file restores only the previously extracted text, not the original file — the raw bytes are never kept anywhere to re-read.

API keys

How a provider API key you add is protected depends on where it's stored:

  • Signed-in accounts — your key is encrypted at rest and can only be decrypted for your own requests. Settings only ever shows a short hint of a saved key, never the key itself.
  • Not signed in — your key stays in your browser's local storage and is sent only with your own comparison requests from that browser.

Either way, your key is only ever sent onward to the AI vendor you chose it for, to run the semantic analysis you requested — never to any other vendor, and never to another user's requests.

Your sign-in session

Signing in with Google creates a session identified by a secure, signed cookie — it doesn't carry your Google password or any Google credential, just enough for DiffFind to recognize your account on later requests. Signing out clears it.

What AI providers see

When semantic analysis runs with a real provider (Claude, OpenAI, or Gemini), the relevant change content and your API key for that provider are sent to that vendor's API to generate the interpretation. That exchange is governed by the vendor's own terms and privacy policy, not DiffFind's — choose the Heuristic provider if you'd rather nothing leave DiffFind's own server at all. See Choosing a provider.

Requesting deletion

To have your account, saved provider keys, or other associated data deleted, contact us from the email address on your account. See also Account & Settings.
